# hack-team-06

An AI-enabled transformation roadmap tool for Aberdeen Advisors' transformation engagements. It
carries an engagement through the firm's eight-phase methodology in one place — charter and
mobilization, fact base, maturity assessment, scored opportunity register, roadmap sequencing and
scenarios, investment and capacity modelling, business alignment and decision log, narrative and
deliverable generation, and the living roadmap after handover — replacing the spreadsheet-and-deck
pipeline where the analysis currently lives and leaks. Every derived number is owned by a
calculation engine rather than typed into a cell, every claim traces back to the evidence behind
it, and thirty-four AI capabilities propose work that a consultant reviews and commits.

The full specification is in [docs/PRD.md](docs/PRD.md).
